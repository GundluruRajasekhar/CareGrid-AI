Medicines

Emergency guide

Call your emergency number now for chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs (face droop, arm weakness, slurred speech), heavy bleeding, seizures or loss of consciousness. India 112 or 108, US 911, UK 999.

General first aid

  • Stay calm, sit or lie down, and unlock the door.
  • Chest pain: chew one regular aspirin only if you are not allergic and a clinician has not told you otherwise.
  • Stroke signs: note the time symptoms started and do not give food or drink.
  • Bleeding: press firmly on the wound with clean cloth.
